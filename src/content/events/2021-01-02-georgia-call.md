In an hour-long call set up by his chief of staff, Mark Meadows, Trump pressed
Georgia's secretary of state, Brad Raffensperger, a fellow Republican, to
overturn Joe Biden's victory in the state. "I just want to find 11,780 votes,"
he said. Georgia had counted its ballots three times and certified Biden's win
by 11,779 votes. Raffensperger refused and told Trump his data was wrong. His
office had recorded the call.
