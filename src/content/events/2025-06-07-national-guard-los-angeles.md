After protests against immigration raids in Los Angeles, Trump took control of
about 4,000 California National Guard members against the wishes of Governor
Gavin Newsom, and the Pentagon sent 700 active-duty Marines. California sued. A
federal judge found that the protests fell "far short of rebellion" and ordered
control of the Guard returned to the state. An appeals court let Trump keep
command while the case continued.
