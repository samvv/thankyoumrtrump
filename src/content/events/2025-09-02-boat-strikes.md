The military began striking small boats in the Caribbean, and later the eastern
Pacific, that the administration says were carrying drugs. It has not provided
definitive evidence that the boats were trafficking. More than 200 people have
been killed. Legal experts have said the strikes are illegal, and some
lawmakers have said a follow-up strike that killed survivors of the first
attack may have been a war crime. The administration says the United States is
in an armed conflict with drug cartels and that the strikes are lawful.
