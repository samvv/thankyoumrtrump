A week into his presidency, Trump signed an order barring entry from Iran,
Iraq, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, Syria and Yemen for 90 days. It also suspended all
refugee admissions for 120 days and barred Syrian refugees indefinitely. Tens
of thousands of visas were revoked before a federal judge halted the order a
week later. The Supreme Court upheld a revised version in 2018 by five votes to
four.
