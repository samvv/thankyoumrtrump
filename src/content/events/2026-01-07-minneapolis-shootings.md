During Operation Metro Surge, a large federal immigration operation in the Twin
Cities, an ICE officer shot and killed Renee Good, a 37-year-old mother of
three, on 7 January 2026. On 24 January, Border Patrol officers shot and killed
Alex Pretti, a 37-year-old intensive care nurse, while he was filming them with
his phone. Federal officials said the officer who shot Good acted in
self-defense; bystander video challenged that account. No criminal charges have
been filed, and Minnesota has sued the federal government, saying it is
withholding evidence from state investigators.
