In the fifth week of the war with Iran, Trump's defense secretary asked General
Randy George, the Army's chief of staff, to step down, and removed two other
senior Army officers. In October he fired Lieutenant General Brett Sylvia, the
Joint Chiefs' top strategic planner, while Trump was weighing new strikes on
Iran. By then nearly two dozen generals, admirals and other military leaders
had been fired, forced to retire or otherwise pushed out under Hegseth, almost
all without a public explanation.
