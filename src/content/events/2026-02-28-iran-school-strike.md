On the first day of the war with Iran, a strike destroyed a girls' elementary
school in the southern city of Minab. Iranian officials put the death toll at
about 170; other counts give at least 156 dead, 120 of them children. Trump at
first blamed Iran. A preliminary Pentagon assessment found that the United
States was likely responsible and had relied on outdated intelligence that
marked the site as a military target. UN-backed experts later said there were
reasonable grounds to believe the strike was a war crime. The administration
has not formally accepted responsibility or released its findings.
