Attorney General Jeff Sessions directed federal prosecutors to criminally
charge every adult caught crossing the southern border illegally. Parents were
jailed and their children were placed in separate government custody. Thousands
of families were split up before Trump ended the practice by executive order on
20 June 2018, and reuniting them took years.
