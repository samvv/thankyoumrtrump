The administration sent more than 230 men, most of them Venezuelans, to the
CECOT prison in El Salvador, many under the Alien Enemies Act, a wartime law.
Among them was Kilmar Abrego Garcia, a Maryland resident whom an immigration
judge had protected from deportation to El Salvador in 2019. The government
admitted in court that his removal was an "administrative error". The Supreme
Court ordered the administration to facilitate his release. He was returned in
June 2025 to face smuggling charges, and his lawyers deny the government's
claim that he is a gang member.
