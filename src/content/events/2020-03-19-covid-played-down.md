On 7 February 2020, Trump told the journalist Bob Woodward that the new
coronavirus was "deadly stuff". In public he went on comparing it to the
seasonal flu. On 19 March he told Woodward: "I wanted to always play it down. I
still like playing it down, because I don't want to create a panic." The
recordings became public in September 2020. Trump responded that he had not
lied to the American people.
