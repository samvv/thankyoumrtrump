The Pan American Health Organization revoked a status the United States had
held since 2000, after finding that measles had spread without interruption
for more than a year. The resurgence began with an outbreak in West Texas in
January 2025. More than 5,400 cases and almost 600 hospitalizations were
confirmed in 2026, and vaccination among kindergartners has fallen to 92%,
below the 95% needed to prevent outbreaks. The health department under
Robert F. Kennedy Jr. says the gaps in vaccination opened
before this administration. Critics point to its response to the outbreaks.
