Trump refused to sign spending bills that did not include money for a wall on
the Mexican border, having said beforehand that he would be "proud to shut down
the government for border security". About 800,000 federal workers went
without pay. On 25 January 2019 he signed a bill that reopened the government
without any wall funding. It was the longest shutdown in US history until a
43-day shutdown in his second term.
