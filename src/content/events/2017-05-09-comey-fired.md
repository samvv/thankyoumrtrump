Trump dismissed James Comey while Comey was overseeing the FBI's investigation
into whether Trump's associates had coordinated with Russia's interference in
the 2016 election. The White House cited Comey's handling of the Hillary
Clinton email inquiry. It was only the second time an FBI director had been
fired. Eight days later the Justice Department appointed Robert Mueller as
special counsel to take over the investigation.
