In a Rose Garden speech, Trump announced that the United States, the world's
second-largest emitter of greenhouse gases, would leave the Paris Agreement.
The withdrawal took formal effect on 4 November 2020. The United States
rejoined in 2021, and Trump ordered a second withdrawal on his first day back
in office in January 2025.
