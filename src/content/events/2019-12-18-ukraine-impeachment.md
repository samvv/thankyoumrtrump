In a July 2019 phone call, Trump asked Ukraine's president, Volodymyr
Zelensky, to investigate Joe Biden, his likely opponent in the 2020 election,
while his administration was withholding nearly $400 million in military aid
from Ukraine. The House impeached him for abuse of power, by 230 votes to 197,
and for obstruction of Congress, by 229 to 198. The Senate acquitted him in
February 2020.
