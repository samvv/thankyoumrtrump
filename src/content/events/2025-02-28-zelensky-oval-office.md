In front of television cameras, Trump and Vice President JD Vance berated
Volodymyr Zelensky, with Trump accusing him of "gambling with millions of
lives". The visit was cut short and a planned minerals deal went unsigned.
Three days later Trump paused US military aid to Ukraine, and the United States
also stopped sharing intelligence. Both resumed about a week later.
