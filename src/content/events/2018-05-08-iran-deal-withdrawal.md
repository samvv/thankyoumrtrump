Trump withdrew from the 2015 agreement under which Iran had limited its nuclear
program in exchange for sanctions relief, and ordered sanctions reimposed. He
offered no evidence that Iran had violated the deal. The leaders of Britain,
France and Germany, who had urged him to stay, expressed "regret and concern".
Iran began exceeding the deal's limits on uranium enrichment the following
year.
