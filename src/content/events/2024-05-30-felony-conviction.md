A Manhattan jury found Trump guilty on all 34 counts of falsifying business
records to conceal a hush-money payment made before the 2016 election. He
became the first former US president to be convicted of a felony. In January
2025 he was sentenced to an unconditional discharge, which carries no prison
time or fine. He is appealing the conviction.
