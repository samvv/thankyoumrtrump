Three days after a white nationalist rally in Charlottesville, Virginia, at
which a man drove a car into a crowd of counterprotesters and killed Heather
Heyer, Trump told reporters there was "blame on both sides" and that there were
"very fine people on both sides". In the same remarks he said neo-Nazis and
white nationalists "should be condemned totally". Leaders of both parties
criticized him, and House Speaker Paul Ryan said the remarks sounded like moral
equivocation.
