Seven months before the midterm elections, Trump signed an order telling the
Department of Homeland Security to compile lists of eligible citizens in each
state and the Postal Service to deliver mail ballots only to people on them.
The Constitution leaves the running of elections to the states and Congress.
Twenty-three states and the District of Columbia sued, and a federal judge
ruled the provisions unconstitutional. In September the Supreme Court blocked
the restrictions from taking effect for the November elections.
