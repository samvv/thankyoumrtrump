Trump's health secretary removed all 17 members of the Advisory Committee on
Immunization Practices, the expert panel that recommends which vaccines
Americans should get, and named his own replacements two days later. He called
it a "clean sweep" needed to restore public confidence. The American Medical
Association said the move "upends a transparent process that has saved
countless lives".
