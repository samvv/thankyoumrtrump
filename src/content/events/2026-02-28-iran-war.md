US and Israeli forces attacked Iran on 28 February 2026, killing its supreme
leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. Iran retaliated against Israel and neighboring
Gulf states and disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Oil rose from
about $70 a barrel before the war to a peak above $126 in April. Trump declared
a ceasefire on 7 April, but strikes later resumed and no agreement to end the
war has been reached. By mid-November 2026 the Pentagon counted 23 American
service members dead and put the cost at more than $48 billion.
