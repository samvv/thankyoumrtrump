Days after a US grand jury indicted 12 Russian intelligence officers for
interfering in the 2016 election, Trump stood beside Vladimir Putin and said the
Russian president had been "extremely strong and powerful in his denial". Of
the interference he said: "I don't see any reason why it would be" Russia.
Senator John McCain called it "one of the most disgraceful performances by an
American president in memory". Trump said the next day that he had misspoken.
