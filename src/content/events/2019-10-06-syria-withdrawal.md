After a phone call between Trump and Turkey's president, Recep Tayyip Erdoğan,
the White House announced that US forces in northeastern Syria would move
aside. Within days Turkey attacked the Kurdish-led forces that had fought the
Islamic State alongside American troops. Trump then ordered a withdrawal of US
forces from northern Syria. Members of his own party condemned the decision,
and American soldiers told reporters they had betrayed an ally.
