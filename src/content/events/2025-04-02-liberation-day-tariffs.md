Trump announced a 10% tariff on most imports and far higher rates on dozens of
trading partners. The S&P 500 lost more than 12% in the week that followed,
before he suspended the higher rates for 90 days. In February 2026 the Supreme
Court ruled, by six votes to three, that the emergency-powers law he had relied
on does not let a president impose tariffs. The government has since been
refunding tens of billions of dollars to importers.
