Hours after returning to office, Trump granted clemency to about 1,500 people
charged over the attack of 6 January 2021, including those convicted of
assaulting police officers. He commuted the sentences of 14 members of the
Proud Boys and Oath Keepers and ordered roughly 300 pending cases dropped.
