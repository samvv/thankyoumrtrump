After a rally at which Trump repeated his false claim that the 2020 election
had been stolen, his supporters broke into the Capitol to stop Congress from
certifying Joe Biden's victory. Some chanted "hang Mike Pence". Federal
prosecutors went on to charge more than 1,500 people. The House impeached Trump
a week later for incitement of insurrection. The Senate voted 57–43 to convict,
short of the two-thirds majority required.
