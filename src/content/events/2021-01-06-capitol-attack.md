After a rally at which Trump repeated his false claim that the 2020 election
had been stolen, his supporters broke into the Capitol to stop Congress from
certifying Joe Biden's victory. The House impeached Trump a week later for
incitement of insurrection. The Senate voted 57–43 to convict, short of the
two-thirds majority required.
