Trump froze foreign aid on his first day in office, and Elon Musk's Department
of Government Efficiency then dismantled the US Agency for International
Development. Its remaining work was folded into the State Department on 1 July
2025. A study published in The Lancet estimated that the cuts could lead to
more than 14 million additional deaths by 2030, about 4.5 million of them
children under five. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has said that no one has
died because of the cuts.
